Free countryball maker

Countryball Studio lets you make your own countryball in a few seconds, right in your browser. Pick a real flag from 265 countries and regions, choose a classic ball or a special shape like a cube, triangle or brick, then add eyes, hats and speech bubbles. When you are done, download it as a PNG with a transparent or colored background. No sign-up, and it works on phones too.

How to make a countryball

  1. Choose a shape on the left: ball, cube, triangle or brick.
  2. Search for a country on the right and tap Apply. Use Upside down or Mirror if your countryball needs it.
  3. Open Face & Extras and add eyes. Drag them where you want, resize with the corner handle.
  4. Add a hat, a mustache or a speech bubble with your own text.
  5. Press Download and save your countryball as a PNG.

Questions

Is it free? Yes, completely free.

Can I use my own flag? Yes. Open "Flag placement" and upload any image.

Does it work offline? Yes. After the first visit you can install it as an app and use it without internet.
